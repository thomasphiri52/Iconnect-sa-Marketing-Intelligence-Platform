# iConnect SA V13 — Enterprise Customer 360 & CRM Intelligence

V13 adds a unified customer intelligence layer connecting account health, revenue, customer lifetime value, churn risk, expansion potential and sales pipeline.

## Features
- Customer 360 profiles
- Customer segmentation
- Account health scoring
- Churn-risk intelligence
- Revenue and CLV analysis
- Expansion/cross-sell scoring
- Sales pipeline intelligence
- CRM integration framework
- Salesforce / HubSpot / Dynamics / REST-ready connector contracts

## Production next steps
Connect a real CRM, persist customer activity in PostgreSQL, add identity-aware account access, event-driven customer scoring and production ML churn/CLV models.
