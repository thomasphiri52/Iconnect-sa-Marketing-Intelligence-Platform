# iConnect SA V12 — Enterprise Collaboration & Notification Centre

V12 adds the collaboration and governance layer required to turn intelligence into coordinated executive action.

## Features
- Central notification centre
- Approval and decision inbox
- Team workspace overview
- Report and alert distribution
- Activity feed
- In-app, email, Teams, Slack and webhook-ready channels
- Escalation actions
- Approval lifecycle
- Operational messaging
- Delivery/security integration contracts

## Production hardening
Connect real identity/RBAC, encrypted secrets, SMTP/OAuth providers, Teams/Slack APIs, signed webhooks, retry queues, delivery receipts and immutable audit storage.
