# V10 Executive Strategy & Scenario Simulator

V10 adds an executive planning layer.

## Added
- Best/base/downside scenarios
- Revenue and profit projections
- ROI modelling
- Investment sensitivity
- Competitive-pressure modelling
- Provincial expansion simulator
- Board decision workflow
- Executive scenario comparison

## Design principle
The models are transparent and deterministic so executives can see and challenge
assumptions. Later versions can connect these assumptions to V8 AI predictions,
V9 live signals and V6 persistent historical data.
