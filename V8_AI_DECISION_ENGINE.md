# V8 AI Decision & Intelligence Engine

V8 adds an explainable decision layer to iConnect SA.

## Capabilities
- Opportunity scoring
- Competitor threat scoring
- Customer churn-risk scoring
- Trend forecasting
- Explainable intelligence signals
- Recommended actions
- Executive AI brief generation

The current engine is deterministic and transparent. It is intentionally designed
as a safe foundation that can later connect to an LLM, ML models and real-time
enterprise data without changing the dashboard contract.
